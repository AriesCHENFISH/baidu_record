History And Evolution

Early Concept

The idea of a virtual world where people can interact digitally was first imagined in science fiction during the early 1990s. It introduced the concept of avatars and shared digital spaces.

Virtual Worlds

In the 2000s, virtual platforms and multiplayer online games began offering immersive environments, allowing users to explore, build, and socialize in digital spaces.

Into Reality

Over time, advances in internet speed, 3D technology, and immersive tools like VR/AR transformed the metaverse from fiction to a developing digital frontier used for work, entertainment, and education.
