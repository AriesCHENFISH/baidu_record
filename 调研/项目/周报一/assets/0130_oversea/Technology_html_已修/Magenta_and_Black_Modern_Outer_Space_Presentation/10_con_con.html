Scorching Orbits

There are regions in space with such immense gravitational pull that they affect the motion of entire galaxies. These 'attractors,' often hidden behind vast clouds of dust and gas, exert a mysterious force on surrounding galactic structures.

Scientists believe that these regions may contain vast concentrations of mass, possibly including superclusters of galaxies or even entirely new forms of matter.
