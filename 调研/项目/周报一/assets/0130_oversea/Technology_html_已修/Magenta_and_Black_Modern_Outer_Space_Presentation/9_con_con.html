Scorching Orbits

Some giant planets, known as 'hot Jupiters,' orbit extremely close to their host stars, defying traditional formation models.

  • Complete full orbits in just a few Earth days due to proximity.
  • Experience scorching surface temperatures and intense stellar radiation.
  • Can become tidally locked, with one side perpetually facing the star.
Exoplanet Classification: Type IV
