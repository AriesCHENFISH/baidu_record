Galactic Crashes

Galaxies are not static islands in space; they interact and collide over vast cosmic timescales. These galactic crashes can trigger bursts of star formation, reshape galactic structures, and even merge galaxies into entirely new forms. Studying these collisions provides valuable insights into the distribution of dark matter and the evolution of the universe.
