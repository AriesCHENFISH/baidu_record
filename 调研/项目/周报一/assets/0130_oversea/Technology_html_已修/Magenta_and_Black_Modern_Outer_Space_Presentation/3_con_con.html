Worlds Made of Strange Stuff

Imagine planets forged from materials far different from the rocky and gaseous worlds of our solar system. Some may be composed almost entirely of crystallized carbon, essentially giant diamonds.

Others might be rich in heavy metals, giving them incredible densities and unique, reflective surfaces. These exotic compositions challenge our understanding of planetary formation.
