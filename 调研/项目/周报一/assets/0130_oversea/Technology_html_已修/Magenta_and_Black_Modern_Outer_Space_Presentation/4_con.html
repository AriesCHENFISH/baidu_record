Drifters in the Dark

Origin Analysis

Not all planets are bound to stars. Some are ejected through gravitational interactions, becoming 'rogue' planets, adrift in the vast emptiness.

Environmental Status

These worlds experience perpetual night. Without a host star's warmth, they face extreme cold that challenges our understanding of potential habitability.
