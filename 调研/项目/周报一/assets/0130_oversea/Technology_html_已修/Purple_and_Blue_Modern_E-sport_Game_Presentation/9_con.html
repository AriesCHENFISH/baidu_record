Challenges
in E-Sports

Player Burnout & Mental Health

Professional gamers face intense pressure, leading to exhaustion and anxiety. Training schedules often exceed 12 hours daily, requiring urgent psychological support systems.

Gender Inequality

The industry still faces a significant gender gap in representation and pay. Creating inclusive environments remains a critical hurdle for long-term growth.

Cheating & Fair Play

From aimbots to match-fixing, maintaining competitive integrity is an ongoing battle requiring advanced anti-cheat tech and strict governance.

Stability of Teams

Financial volatility and high operational costs lead to frequent organization closures, highlighting the need for more sustainable business models.
