Definition

The Internet is a global system of interconnected computer networks that use the standard TCP/IP protocol suite to link several billion devices worldwide. It is a network of networks that consists of private, public, academic, business, and government networks.

Brief History

Starting with the ARPANET in 1969, the internet evolved from a military research project into a global communication powerhouse.
