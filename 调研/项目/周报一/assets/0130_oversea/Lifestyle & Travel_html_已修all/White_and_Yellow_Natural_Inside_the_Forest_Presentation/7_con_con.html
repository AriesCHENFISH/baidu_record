Natural Soundscape

Bird calls, rustling leaves, and animal noises create a symphony that reflects life and forest activity. These acoustic signatures provide critical data on ecosystem health.

Monitoring Tools

Scientists analyze these sounds to detect biodiversity, stress, or absence of species over time, utilizing advanced bio-acoustic sensors deployed in remote canopies.
