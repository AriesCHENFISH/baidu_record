Mutualism (Win-Win)

A relationship where both species benefit. For example, bees get nectar from flowers while pollinating them, ensuring the reproduction of the plant.

Commensalism (One Wins)

One organism benefits while the other is neither helped nor harmed. Epiphytes growing on tall trees to reach sunlight is a classic example.

Parasitism (Win-Loss)

One organism (the parasite) benefits at the expense of the other (the host). Mistletoe extracting water and nutrients from a tree is a form of parasitism.
