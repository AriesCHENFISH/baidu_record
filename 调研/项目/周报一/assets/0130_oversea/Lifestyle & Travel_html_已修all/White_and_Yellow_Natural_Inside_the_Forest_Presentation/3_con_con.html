Canopy Layer

The uppermost level where tall trees absorb sunlight and offer shelter to birds, insects, and mammals. It acts as the forest's solar panel.

Understory Layer

Found below the canopy, this layer includes shrubs and small trees that have adapted to low light and high humidity conditions.

Forest Floor

The lowest layer, filled with decomposing matter, fungi, and crawling life. It is essential for breaking down organic material and recycling nutrients.
