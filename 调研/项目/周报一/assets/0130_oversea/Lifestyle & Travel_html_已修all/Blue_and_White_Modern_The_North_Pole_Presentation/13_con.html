01. Adaptation

Permafrost Survivors

Plants like mosses and lichens grow low to the ground, creating miniature forests that resist freezing winds and utilize the thin active soil layer.

02. Growth Cycle

The Midnight Sun Bloom

During the brief summer, the tundra explodes in color as hardy flowers complete their entire reproduction cycle under 24-hour daylight.

03. Ecosystem Role

Global Carbon Sink

The cold, wet tundra soil traps vast amounts of organic carbon for millennia, playing a vital role in regulating Earth's climate.
