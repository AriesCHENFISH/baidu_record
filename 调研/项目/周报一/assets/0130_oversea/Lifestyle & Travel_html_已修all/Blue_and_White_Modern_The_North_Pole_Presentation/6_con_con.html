Chronicle of Exploration

19th Century: Early Expeditions

Explorers from around the world began venturing toward the North Pole, often facing harsh and deadly conditions in wooden vessels.

20th Century: The Great Race

The early 20th century saw competing claims of reaching the Pole, filled with stories of triumph, mystery, and incredible endurance.

Modern Era: Advanced Frontiers

Today, aircraft, nuclear icebreakers, and satellite technology allow safer exploration, though the environment remains untamed.
