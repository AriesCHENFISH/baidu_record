Economic Stakes

Melting Arctic ice is revealing new shipping lanes and potential natural resources, prompting countries to assert influence over this region. The race for control is increasing tension and competition in the high north.

Global treaties and international cooperation are essential to prevent conflict and protect the Arctic. As interest rises, careful diplomacy will determine whether this region is exploited or preserved.
