Forests act as the planet's primary respiratory system, sequestering carbon and exhaling the oxygen that sustains all life. Their majesty lies not only in their ancient endurance but in the complex, hidden networks that bind every tree into a singular, resilient organism.

From the temperate pines to the tropical canopies, our research tracks the health of these vital green cathedrals in an era of rapid environmental transition.
