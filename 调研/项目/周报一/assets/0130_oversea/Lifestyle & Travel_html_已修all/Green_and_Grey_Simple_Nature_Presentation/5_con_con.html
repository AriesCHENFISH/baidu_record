Nature’s silent link

KEY SPECIES: APIS MELLIFERA

Global Impact

More than 75% of the world's leading food crops rely on animal pollination. Bees, butterflies, and birds act as the primary engines of biodiversity, ensuring the survival of our wild ecosystems.

Ecosystem Services

Pollinators contribute substantially to the economy by ensuring the production of fruits, nuts, and seeds. Their decline poses a direct threat to food security and ecological stability worldwide.
