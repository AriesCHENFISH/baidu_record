What are Mountains?

Defining
Mountains

Mountains are significant landforms that rise substantially above their surrounding areas. They are characterized by their elevation, slope, and local relief.

High Relief

Elevated landforms with a high degree of relief.

Steep Slopes

Typically have steep slopes and a defined summit.

Geological Origin

Formed by various geological processes.
