Ecosystem Services

Carbon Sequestration

Plants act as a global carbon sink, absorbing CO2 from the atmosphere and storing it in biomass and soil, mitigating the greenhouse effect.

Soil Stabilization

Extensive root systems prevent erosion, maintain soil structure, and facilitate the nutrient cycling necessary for all terrestrial agriculture.

Water Regulation

Through transpiration and filtration, plants manage the water cycle, purifying groundwater and regulating the humidity of entire regions.
