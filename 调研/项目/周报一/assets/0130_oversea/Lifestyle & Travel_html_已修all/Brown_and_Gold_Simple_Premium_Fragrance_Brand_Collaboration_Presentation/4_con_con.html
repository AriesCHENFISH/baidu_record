About Us

Crafting Nature's Elegance Since 1997

Welcome to Hanover and Tyke, where the essence of nature meets the artistry of fragrance. Established in 1997, we have been dedicated to curating exquisite perfumes that embody the pure beauty of natural ingredients, ensuring an unparalleled olfactory experience for our discerning customers.

At Hanover and Tyke, we believe in the power of nature's finest elements. Every fragrance in our collection is a testament to our commitment to using exclusively natural ingredients. From the lush floral notes to the earthy undertones, each perfume is a harmonious blend crafted with precision and passion.
