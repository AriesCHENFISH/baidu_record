Scientific Overview

The Lithospheric Evolution of Orogeny

Mountain formation, or orogeny, is a complex geological process primarily driven by the interaction of tectonic plates.

01

Tectonic Folding & Faulting

When two continental plates collide, the crust is compressed and forced upward. This compression causes the rock layers to bend into folds or break into faults, resulting in the creation of fold mountains such as the Himalayas and the Alps.

02

Volcanic Magmatic Accumulation

Oceanic plate subduction forces magma to surface through the crust. Over time, layers of lava and volcanic ash accumulate, building volcanic mountains. This process is highly active in the "Ring of Fire" across the Pacific Basin.
