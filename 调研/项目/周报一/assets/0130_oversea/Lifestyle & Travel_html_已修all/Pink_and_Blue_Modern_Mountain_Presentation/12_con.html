Vertical
Climate Zones

Thermodynamic Principles

Temperature lapse rates dictate that for every 1,000 feet of ascent, the air temperature drops approximately 3.5 degrees Fahrenheit, fundamentally altering soil chemistry and biological capacity.

Ecological Transitions

These transitions create abrupt ecotones—boundaries where one ecosystem meets another—resulting in some of the highest levels of endemic species diversity found on planet Earth.

Climatic Observation

Stratified Biodiversity

Scientific analysis confirms that vertical stratification serves as a natural laboratory for studying climate adaptation, offering insights into how species may respond to global thermal shifts.
