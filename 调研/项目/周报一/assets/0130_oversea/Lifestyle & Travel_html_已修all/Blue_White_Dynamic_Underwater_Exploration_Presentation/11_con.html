OCEAN ZONES

The Vertical Dimension of Life

0-200M Sunlight Zone

The surface layer where sunlight allows photosynthesis to occur. This zone is home to 90% of marine life, including coral reefs and vast schools of colorful fish thriving in warm, bright waters.

200-1000M Twilight Zone

Light fades rapidly here, and pressure begins to increase. Creatures in this transitional layer often have large eyes or bioluminescent features to navigate the dim and mysterious environment.

1000M+ Midnight Zone

The deep trenches where sunlight never reaches. Life survives on "marine snow" and geothermal heat. It remains the most mysterious and least explored region of our entire planet.
