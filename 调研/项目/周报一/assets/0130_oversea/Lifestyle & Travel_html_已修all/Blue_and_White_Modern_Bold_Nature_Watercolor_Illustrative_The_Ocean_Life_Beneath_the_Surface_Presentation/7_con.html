Ocean Currents

Ocean currents are continuous, directed movements of seawater generated by a number of forces acting upon this mean flow, such as breaking waves, wind, the Coriolis effect, cabbeling, and temperature differences.

Depth contours, shoreline configurations, and interactions with other currents influence a current's direction and strength. These currents are critical in determining the climate of many of Earth's regions.
