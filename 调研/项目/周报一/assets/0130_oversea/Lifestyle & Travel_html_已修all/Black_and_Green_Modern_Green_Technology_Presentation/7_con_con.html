What is a Sustainable Building?

A sustainable building is designed and constructed to minimize environmental impact while maximizing efficiency and occupant well-being. It integrates renewable energy, efficient resource use, and environmentally friendly materials.

Benefits of Sustainable Buildings

The benefits include reduced energy consumption, lower operational costs, improved indoor air quality, and enhanced resilience against environmental changes, making buildings more sustainable and cost-effective.
