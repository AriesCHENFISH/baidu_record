What is Green Transportation?

Green transportation includes modes of travel that have minimal environmental impact by reducing carbon emissions and energy consumption. It includes electric, hybrid, and fuel-efficient vehicles, as well as public transportation and non-motorized options like biking.

Electric Vehicles (EVs)

Cars powered by electricity instead of gasoline, reducing emissions and reliance on fossil fuels.

Public Transportation

Buses, trains, and subways that move large numbers of people efficiently, reducing individual footprints.

Bicycles and E-Scooters

Eco-friendly alternatives that produce zero emissions and promote healthier lifestyles.
