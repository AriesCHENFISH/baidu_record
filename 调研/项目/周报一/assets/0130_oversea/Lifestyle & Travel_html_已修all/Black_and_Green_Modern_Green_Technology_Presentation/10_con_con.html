Challenges and Future
of Green Technology

Challenges

  • High Initial Costs

    Many green technologies require significant investment before long-term benefits are realized.

  • Limited Public Awareness

    The adoption of green technology is hindered by a lack of awareness and understanding.

Future of Green Technology

  • Advancements in Renewable Energy

    Continuous innovation will make renewable energy more efficient and cost-effective.

  • Government Policies and Incentives

    Stronger regulations and financial incentives will promote widespread adoption.
