Introduction

A New Frontier in Technology

Quantum computing harnesses the phenomena of quantum mechanics to deliver a huge leap forward in computation to solve certain problems. Unlike classical computers, which switch transistors on and off to symbolize data as ones or zeros, quantum computers use qubits which can exist in multiple states simultaneously.

This superposition allows quantum algorithms to process information at speeds unimaginable with today's supercomputers. As we approach the physical limits of Moore's Law, this technology promises to revolutionize fields from cryptography and drug discovery to financial modeling and climate prediction.
