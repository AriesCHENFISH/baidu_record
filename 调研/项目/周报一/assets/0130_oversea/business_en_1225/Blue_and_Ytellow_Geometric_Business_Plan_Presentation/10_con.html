Product Strategy MVP Roadmap

A systematic six-step approach to validating hypotheses and building products that deliver real customer value.

1

Identify Target Customer

Use market segmentation and create personas. Form a high-level hypothesis.

2

Know Underserved Needs

Identify gaps in market solutions. What needs are adequately met vs ignored?

3

Define Value Prop

How will you meet needs better than competitors? Focus on key differentiators.

4

State MVP Features

Build only what creates enough value to validate the product direction.

5

Make MVP Prototype

Create a version to test hypotheses. Apply UX principles to bring it to life.

6

Test With Customers

Gather feedback from your target market to iterate in the right direction.
