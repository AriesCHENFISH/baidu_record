Marketing Funnel Stages

A strategic model that illustrates the theoretical customer journey toward the purchase of a good or service, from initial evaluation to final conversion.

MOFU (Middle of the Funnel)

Activities that facilitate evaluation. Convert those who are aware into leads through engagement and education.

  • Discounts
  • Exclusive offers
  • Free trial
  • Event

BOFU (Bottom of the Funnel)

Activities that facilitate conversion. Guide the qualified leads in making an informed purchase decision.

  • Demo
  • Feedback
  • Success stories
  • Comparison sheets
