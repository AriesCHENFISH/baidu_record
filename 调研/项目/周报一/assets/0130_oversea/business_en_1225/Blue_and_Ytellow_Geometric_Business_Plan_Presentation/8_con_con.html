The 4Ps of Marketing

Product

  • Brand
  • Features
  • Packaging

Product refers to the good or service you are offering to customers. What needs does this product fulfil? What frustrations does it address? What makes it compelling?

Price

  • Price
  • Discounts
  • Bundling
  • Credit

Price is what customers pay for a product or service. What is the value to customers? Are there established price points? How will this compare with competitors?

Promotion

  • Ads
  • PR
  • Social
  • Email
  • SEO

Promotion is how you will get the word out about your product or service to your target customers. It includes advertising, public relations, and promotional strategies.

Place

  • Stores
  • Website
  • Online
  • Marketplace

Place refers to how and where your customers will look to see your product. Will it be in a physical store or online? What will be the distribution channels?
