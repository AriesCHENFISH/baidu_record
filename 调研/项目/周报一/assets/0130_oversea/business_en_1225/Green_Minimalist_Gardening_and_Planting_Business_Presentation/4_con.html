Care Guide

Optimal Care Conditions

Sunlight Exposure

Most flowering plants require at least 6 hours of direct sunlight daily to bloom profusely. Place them in south or west-facing locations for best results. Shade-loving varieties are exceptions.

Watering Schedule

Consistent moisture is key, but avoid waterlogging. Check soil dampness before watering; the top inch should be dry. Morning watering is preferred to allow foliage to dry before nightfall.
