/// 01. CORE CONCEPT

Redefining the
Visual Language.

The acid graphics aesthetic prioritizes high contrast, experimental typography, and a rejection of traditional grid rigidity. This section explores how we integrate these chaotic elements into functional UI.

SYSTEM_ANALYSIS_V2.0

Implementation Strategy

  • 01
    Grid Systems

    While the aesthetic feels chaotic, the underlying structure relies on a strict 12-column grid to ensure readability across devices.

  • 02
    Typography Hierarchy

    Utilizing Noto Sans SC with variable weights ranging from 300 to 900. Heavy contrast between headings and body text is mandatory.

  • 03
    Color Calibration

    Primary Lime (#DFFF5E) used strictly for navigation and emphasis. Charcoal (#111111) serves as the infinite canvas.
