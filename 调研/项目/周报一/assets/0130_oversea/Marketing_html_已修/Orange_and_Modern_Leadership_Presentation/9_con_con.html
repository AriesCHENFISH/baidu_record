The heart of leadership is service. Great leaders put the needs of others ahead of their own. They lead not to gain power, but to create value and positive change. Servant leadership is rooted in empathy, generosity, and responsibility. It focuses on the well-being, success, and growth of others. This mindset builds teams that are strong, loyal, and motivated. Leadership becomes most effective when it is an act of giving, not taking.
