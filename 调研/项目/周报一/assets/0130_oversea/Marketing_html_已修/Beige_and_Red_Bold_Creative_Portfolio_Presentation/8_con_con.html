The Challenge

Developing a cohesive visual language that transcends cultural boundaries. We focused on the intersection of brutalist architecture and digital fluidity, creating a framework that scales from mobile devices to large-scale environmental graphics.

The Solution

By leveraging a strict 12-column grid and a limited monochromatic palette with high-contrast accents, we achieved a timeless aesthetic that prioritizes content legibility without sacrificing the brand's bold creative identity.
