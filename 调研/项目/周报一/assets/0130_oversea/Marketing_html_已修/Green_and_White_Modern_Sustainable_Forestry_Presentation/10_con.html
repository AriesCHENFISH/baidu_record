The Deforestation
Crisis

Annual Loss Estimate

10 Million Hectares per year

Primary Drivers of Loss

01

Agricultural Expansion

Conversion of forests for commodities like palm oil, soy, and cattle ranching accounts for the largest share of deforestation globally.

02

Unsustainable Logging

Both legal and illegal timber extraction degrades vast forest areas, opening paths for further clearing and degradation.

03

Infrastructure Projects

The construction of roads, dams, and mining operations fragments ecosystems and provides access to previously remote areas.
