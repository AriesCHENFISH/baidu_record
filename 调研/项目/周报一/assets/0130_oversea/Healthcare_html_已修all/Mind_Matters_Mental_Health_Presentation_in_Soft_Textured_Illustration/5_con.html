Myths & Misconceptions

The Myth

"Mental illness is a sign of personal weakness or character flaw."

The Reality

It is a medical health issue driven by biological, genetic, and environmental factors.

The Reality

Half of all mental health conditions start by age 14, and early intervention is vital.

The Myth

"People with mental health conditions are violent and unpredictable."

The Reality

Statistically, they are far more likely to be victims of violence than perpetrators.

The Reality

Professional therapy and support are proven, effective paths to long-term recovery.
