Vitality Essentials

Role of Nutrition

Macronutrients

Proteins, carbohydrates, and healthy fats provide the fundamental energy required for cellular repair and daily bodily functions.

Micronutrients

Essential vitamins and minerals act as catalysts for metabolic processes, supporting bone health and immune response.

Dietary Balance

A diverse intake of whole foods ensures a stable blood sugar level and prevents metabolic syndromes over the long term.

Hydration Role

Water is crucial for nutrient transport and temperature regulation, making it the most vital "nutrient" for cognitive clarity.
