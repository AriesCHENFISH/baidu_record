Importance of Physical Activity

  • Exercise strengthens the heart, muscles, and bones for better physical function.

  • Physical activity improves circulation and helps maintain a healthy body weight.

  • Simple movements like walking or stretching offer lasting health benefits.

Regular physical activity improves cardiovascular function, strengthens muscles and bones, and promotes healthy body weight. It also supports circulation and enhances the body’s ability to recover from illness or injury. Activities like walking, stretching, and cycling can be easily added to daily routines, offering long-term benefits without the need for intense workouts.
