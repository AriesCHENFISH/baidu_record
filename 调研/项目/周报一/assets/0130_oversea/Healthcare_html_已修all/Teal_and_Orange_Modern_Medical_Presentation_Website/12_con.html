Infection Control

Respiratory Etiquette

Coughing and sneezing spread droplets that can carry viruses up to 6 feet away. Following proper etiquette is crucial to stop the chain of transmission.

Use a Tissue

Cover your mouth and nose with a tissue when you cough or sneeze. This traps the droplets immediately.

Throw away immediately

Use Your Elbow

If no tissue is available, cough or sneeze into your upper sleeve or elbow, not your hands.

Preferred Method

Sanitize Hands

Always wash your hands with soap and water for 20 seconds after coughing, sneezing, or blowing your nose.

Or use alcohol gel
Never cough into your bare hands!
