01 The 5-Step Technique (WHO Standard)

Wet

Use clean, running water.

Lather

Rub soap on backs of hands.

Scrub

At least 20 seconds.

Rinse

Under clean running water.

Dry

Clean towel or air dry.

Proper handwashing is the single most effective way to stop the spread of infections and protect your community.

Wash before eating to prevent ingestion.

Wash after restroom to eliminate pathogens.

Why Soap?

Soap molecules pry open the lipid outer shell of viruses, effectively destroying them and washing them away.

Warning

Hand sanitizer is a backup, not a replacement for dirty hands.
