Food Safety

Practice Good
Food Hygiene

Proper food handling is the first line of defense against foodborne illnesses. From washing produce to cooking temperatures, small steps make a big difference in health.

Clean

Wash hands and surfaces often. Rinse fruits and veggies.

Separate

Don't cross-contaminate. Keep raw meat apart from other foods.

Cook

Cook to the right temperature to kill harmful bacteria.

Chill

Refrigerate promptly. Bacteria grows fast at room temp.

"When in doubt, throw it out." — Don't risk eating spoiled food.

Storage Tip

Store food properly to maintain freshness and prevent spoilage.
