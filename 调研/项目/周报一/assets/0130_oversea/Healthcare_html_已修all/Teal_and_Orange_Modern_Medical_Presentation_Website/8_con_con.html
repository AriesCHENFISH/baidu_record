Hygiene Safety

Keep Personal
Items Personal

Sharing items like towels, razors, or toothbrushes can transfer bacteria, viruses, and fungi. Even within families, personal hygiene tools should remain single-user to prevent the spread of infections like Staph, cold sores, or ringworm.

Razors & Clippers

Can transmit bloodborne pathogens (Hep B/C) if skin is nicked.

Towels & Linens

Damp fabrics breed bacteria and fungi (Athlete's Foot).

Bar Soap

Bacteria can stay on the surface. Use liquid soap instead.

Makeup

Eye and lip products easily transfer viral infections.

Pro Tip Color-code toothbrushes and towels for each family member to avoid accidental swapping.
