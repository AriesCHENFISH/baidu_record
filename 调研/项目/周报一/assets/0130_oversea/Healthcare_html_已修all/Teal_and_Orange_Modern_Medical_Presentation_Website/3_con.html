Prioritize
Sleep

Sleep is when your immune system releases proteins called cytokines, which help promote sleep and fight infection. Lack of sleep can weaken your body's defense mechanism.

7-9 Hrs Recommended for Adults

Healthy Sleep Habits

Simple changes to improve your immunity.

Limit Screens

Avoid phones and computers 1 hour before bed. Blue light suppresses melatonin.

Consistent Schedule

Go to bed and wake up at the same time every day, even on weekends.

Cool Environment

Keep your bedroom cool (around 65°F or 18°C) for optimal sleep quality.

Limit Caffeine

Avoid caffeine and heavy meals late in the day to prevent sleep disruption.
