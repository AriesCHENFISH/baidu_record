Balanced Diet for Immunity

Ref: USDA / WHO

Daily Plate Composition

A strong immune system relies on a steady supply of nutrients. Experts recommend dividing your daily food intake into these proportions to ensure your body has the fuel to fight off infections and maintain hygiene from the inside out.

Why this balance?

  • Fruits & Veg: Provide essential vitamins (C, A, E) and antioxidants.
  • Protein: Builds and repairs tissue and fights viral/bacterial infections.
