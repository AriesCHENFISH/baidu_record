Personal Grooming

Keep Nails Clean & Trimmed

Trim Regularly

Cut fingernails straight across, then round the tips slightly. This prevents ingrown nails and reduces dirt accumulation.

Scrub with Soap

Every time you wash your hands, scrub the underside of your nails with soap and water (or a nail brush).

Don't Bite

Biting nails creates open wounds for bacteria to enter and transfers germs directly from your hands to your mouth.

Sterilize Tools

Don't share nail clippers. Clean them with alcohol before use to prevent fungal infections.

Did you know?

Fingernails can trap hundreds of thousands of bacteria underneath the tip. Long nails are harder to keep clean than short ones.

Suggestion:
Keep them short.
