Section 01 / Origins

History of

Badminton

Badminton originated in mid-19th century British India, initially known as "Poona." The sport's modern rules were later codified at Badminton House in Gloucestershire. Evolving from an ancient court game to the world's fastest racket sport, it represents the ultimate pursuit of athletic excellence and competitive spirit.
