The Rules

Scoring System

A match consists of the best of 3 games of 21 points. Every time there is a serve – there is a point scored.

Service Rules

Shuttlecock must be hit below the waist height. Server and receiver must stand within diagonally opposite service courts.

Intervals & Change of Ends

When the leading score reaches 11 points, players have a 60 second interval. A 2-minute interval between each game.
