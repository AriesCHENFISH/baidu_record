The Apex
Tower

Location

Shanghai, CN

Completion

2025

Area

120,000 m²

A landmark mixed-use development redefining the city skyline. The design emphasizes structural expressionism, utilizing exposed steel framing and a unique geometric facade to maximize natural light and energy efficiency.

  • LEED Platinum Certification
  • Advanced seismic resistance system
  • Integrated smart building technology

Key Challenge

Balancing the massive structural load with the requirement for column-free interior spaces across 50 floors.
