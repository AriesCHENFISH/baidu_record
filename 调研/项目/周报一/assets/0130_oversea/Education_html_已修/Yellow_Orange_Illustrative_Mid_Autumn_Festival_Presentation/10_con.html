Symbolic Traditions

01

Moon Gazing

Families gather on balconies or hilltops to appreciate the brightest full moon of the year, symbolizing unity and togetherness.

02

Lantern Riddles

Writing riddles on colorful lanterns is a beloved intellectual game that brings laughter and wisdom to the festive night.

03

Osmanthus Wine

The fragrance of osmanthus blooms defines the autumn. Drinking osmanthus wine signifies a sweet, harmonious life and good fortune.
