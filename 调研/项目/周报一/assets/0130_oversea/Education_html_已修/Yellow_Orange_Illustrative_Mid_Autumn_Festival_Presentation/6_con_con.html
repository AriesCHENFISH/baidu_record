The Legend of the
Jade Rabbit

"Deep within the luminescent embrace of the Moon Palace, a celestial companion tirelessly works for the heavens..."

The Jade Rabbit remains one of the most enduring symbols of the Mid-Autumn Festival. Legend says it was chosen for its unparalleled selflessness, earning a place among the stars to accompany the Moon Goddess, Chang'e, for eternity.
