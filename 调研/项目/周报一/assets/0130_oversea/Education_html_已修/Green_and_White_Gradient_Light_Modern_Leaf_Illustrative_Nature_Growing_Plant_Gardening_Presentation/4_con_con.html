Planning Your Garden

Choosing Location

Identify areas with optimal sunlight exposure (6-8 hours) and ensure proximity to a reliable water source for easy maintenance.

What to Grow

Select plants based on your local climate zone and seasonal timing to maximize yield and plant health.

Garden Layout

Sketch a plan considering plant heights and spacing to prevent overcrowding and ensure each variety receives enough air and light.

Care Strategy

Implement companion planting to naturally deter pests and improve soil nutrients through symbiotic plant relationships.
