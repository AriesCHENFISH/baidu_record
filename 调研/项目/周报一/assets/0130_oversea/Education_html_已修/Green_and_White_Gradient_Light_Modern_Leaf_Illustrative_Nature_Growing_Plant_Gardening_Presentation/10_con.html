LARANA GARDEN
Tip 01

Morning Harvest

Plants are most hydrated in the morning. Harvest before the sun gets too high to ensure the longest shelf life and crispest texture.

Tip 02

Color & Size

Don't wait for "giant" produce. Most vegetables are tenderest and most flavorful when they reach their standard mature color and medium size.

The Harvesting Guide

Maximizing your garden's yield requires patience and precise timing. Follow these essential rules for a successful harvest season.

Tip 03

Clean Cuts

Always use sharp, sterilized shears. Tearing stems can invite disease into the "mother plant" and damage the surrounding growth.

Tip 04

Gentle Handling

Fresh produce bruises easily. Use shallow baskets to avoid crushing the bottom layer and move your harvest to a cool place immediately.

Pro Expert Tip

"Successive planting ensures you don't have all your vegetables ready at the exact same time, extending your harvest for months."
