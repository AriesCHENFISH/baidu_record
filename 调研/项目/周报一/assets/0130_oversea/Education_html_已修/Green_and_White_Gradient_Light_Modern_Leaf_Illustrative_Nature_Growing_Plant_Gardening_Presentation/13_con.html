Essential Garden Herbs

Cultivating Flavor and Wellness at Home

Sweet Basil

Known as the "King of Herbs," Basil is essential for Mediterranean cuisine. It thrives in warm, sunny spots and requires consistent moisture.

Garden Rosemary

This woody perennial is highly resilient and drought-tolerant once established. Its needle-like leaves offer a powerful, pine-like fragrance.

Peppermint

Mint is a vigorous grower that prefers partial shade and moist soil. It is incredibly versatile, used in everything from refreshing teas to desserts.

English Thyme

A tiny leaf with a mighty flavor, Thyme is a staple in French cooking. It is a low-maintenance groundcover that loves well-drained soil.
