Court Surfaces

Tennis is played on a variety of surfaces including grass and clay. Each surface affects the speed and bounce of the ball differently.

The Net

The net divides the court into two equal halves. It stands 3 feet (0.914 m) high at the center and 3.5 feet (1.07 m) at the posts.

Service Zones

The service boxes are crucial for starting the point. Serving into the correct box diagonally is the first rule of play.
