Historic Beginnings

The Paralympics began as a small event for injured war veterans. Over time, it evolved into a global celebration of human ability and unity. It stands as a testament to resilience, transforming perceived limitations into boundless potential on the world stage.

Modern Evolution

Today, the games continue to grow, offering a powerful message: that sports belong to everyone, regardless of physical condition or challenges faced. This evolution reflects a broader societal shift towards acceptance, equity, and the recognition of diverse strengths.
