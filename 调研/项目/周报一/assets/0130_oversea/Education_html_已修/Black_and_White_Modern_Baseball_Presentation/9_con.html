Rule Section 6.0

The Strike Zone

Defined Area

Batter's Torso to Knees

The area over home plate the upper limit of which is a horizontal line at the midpoint between the top of the shoulders and the top of the uniform pants, and the lower level is a line at the hollow beneath the kneecap.

Illegal Pitch (Balk)

An illegal act by the pitcher with a runner or runners on base, entitling all runners to advance one base. Usually caused by deceiving motion.

Interference

An act by the team at bat which interferes with, obstructs, impedes, hinders or confuses any fielder attempting to make a play.
