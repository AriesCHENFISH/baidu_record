Rule Book Section 4.0

Standard Inning Flow

9 Innings Total
Phase 01

Top of Inning

The Visiting Team bats (Offense) while the Home Team fields. The goal is to score runs before 3 outs are recorded.

Phase 02

Bottom of Inning

The Home Team bats. If the home team is winning after the top of the 9th, this phase is skipped (Walk-off).

Winning Criteria:
Most Runs Scored
3 Outs per Half-Inning
