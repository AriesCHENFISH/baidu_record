A Brief History of
Women's Football

From the earliest recorded matches to the global phenomenon it is today, women's football has fought for every inch of the pitch. Despite historical bans, the spirit of the game persevered, leading to the professional leagues we celebrate today.

The modern era has seen an explosion in popularity, with record-breaking attendance at World Cups. Investment in grassroots programs ensures that the next generation will have unprecedented opportunities.
