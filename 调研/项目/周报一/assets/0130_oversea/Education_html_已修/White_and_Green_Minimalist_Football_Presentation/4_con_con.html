01 How to win a match

To win, a team must score more goals than the opponent within the 90-minute regulation time. Strategic passing and possession control are crucial for victory.

02 Playing Rules

Fouls result in free kicks or penalties. Severe infractions lead to yellow or red cards, which can significantly alter the game's momentum and team structure.
